# BB1 Section Naming Audit

## Purpose

Rhythm Conductor section buttons should describe musical function, not the drum voice that happens to distinguish one pattern from another. A player should be able to choose a useful next section without already knowing the source transcription.

This audit changes labels only. It does not change notes, timing, kits, Dillo lines, ordering or catalogue identity.

## Naming rules

1. Prefer `Intro`, `Main`, `Verse`, `Chorus`, `Bridge`, `Breakdown`, `Fill`, `Ending` and `Variation` when the source evidence supports that role.
2. When the musical role has not been established, use neutral `Main Variation`, `Alternate` or `Part` labels rather than inventing a verse or chorus.
3. Keep a meaningful written ending marker, but normalize forms such as `(end)` to `Ending`.
4. Do not expose implementation differences such as `Kick`, `Snare`, `Rimshot`, `Clap` or `Tom` in a named-song performance label.
5. Preserve historically meaningful voice/style names in the CR-8000, CR-78, TR-66 and TR-77 preset shelves. Those are machine patterns, not claimed song sections.
6. Keep labels short enough for the 240 px Conductor tiles. The renderer may abbreviate for fit, but the catalogue name should remain understandable.

## Requires musical review before renaming

- Whether Tainted Love's five rimshot-derived patterns are verse, chorus or bridge variants rather than neutral main variations.
- Whether its three clap-derived patterns are breakdowns, choruses or alternates.
- The unnamed `1` section in You Spin Me Round.
- Generic `Part 1`, `Part 2` and numbered sections elsewhere in the catalogue. These are unclear, but a confident role cannot be derived from their current names alone.

## Release gate

Before a label batch enters the library:

- compare every changed section's musical data before and after repacking;
- prove only the intended catalogue name records changed;
- render every affected Conductor page and reject clipped or ambiguous labels;
- run the full catalogue reader, Conductor reachability and library-range checks;
- listen through the changed songs on the instrument before treating role-specific names as accepted.
